# Inspirations — what Mipo's world looks and plays like

status: reference

The pictures, toys and apps behind Mipo, and what to take from each. Add one when it comes up.

## Look

- **Childcraft** (the 1970s children's encyclopedias). Nikki owns the whole series. The
  hand-illustrated 70s look and tone are the reference for the Mipo universe as a whole.
- **James Gulliver Hancock, *Underworld***: https://jamesgulliverhancock.com/projects/underworld/
  A side view under the grass: naked mole-rats in the tunnels, worms in a pale top soil, a potato
  hanging its roots into darker earth with bones and stones. The picture behind the burrow
  (`BURROW.md`). Nikki's 2022 trace of it: `resources/svg_to_love/molletjes.svg` (vector-sketch root).
- **Roy Scholten, *50 vogels***: https://grafiek.royscholten.nl/project/50-vogels/ (at the top of
  `../scrolling-world/main.lua`).

## Play

- **Fabuland** (Lego, 1979-89). A big inspiration: big chunky parts you snap together quickly to
  make a house, a windmill, a well. Building in Mipo's world (`WORLD-BUILDING.md` 1f).
- **Sago Mini**. How it feels to play: one verb, no fail, the camera follows, sound makes it
  alive. The scope rule is named after it (the Sago Rule, `../MANIFESTO.md`).
- **Tinybop**: their brand article is in `../scrolling-world/designdocs/readme.txt`.
- **King Rollo** (1980, David McKee): the spark for a season of Mipo shorts (`KING-ROLLO.md`).

## Palettes

vector-sketch's `src/palettes.lua` (repo root) has palettes from several of these and more:
`fabuland`, `jamesGulliverHancock` (~90 colours), `childCraft`, `quentinBlake`, `miffy`, `lego`,
`littleGreene`, `pico`, `gruvBox`. Playtime's own editor palette is `src/color.lua`.
