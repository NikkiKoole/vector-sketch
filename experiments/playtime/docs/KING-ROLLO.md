# King Rollo: the notebook for a season of Mipo shorts

status: idea

**Started:** 2026-10-02. **Status:** watching + collecting.

**The spark:** *King Rollo* (1980, David McKee, King Rollo Films, narrated by
Ray Brooks). 13 five-minute episodes made from picture books by the author's
own tiny studio. The look, the sound, the storytelling, the silliness: all of
it. The plan is to watch every episode, keep notes, then write a **season of
Mipo shorts**. Each short is meant to become a small game/app **and** a video.

**Why it fits:**
- **Cut-out animation = puppetmaker.** Flat painted parts with outlines,
  jointed, very little motion. That's the Mipo ragdoll with its bg/fg/line layers.
- **One small trouble per episode = the Sago Rule** (`../MANIFESTO.md`): one
  verb, one resolution beat.
- **Books to shorts, by one small studio.** That's the format path in
  `STUDIO-STRATEGY.md` (short video, then picture book), already done once.

## Part 1: Watch notes (one card per King Rollo episode)

Copy this block for each episode.

```
### Ep N: <title>
- The trouble:     (what goes wrong, one sentence; usually tiny)
- The silly turn:  (where it gets absurd, and roughly when: min 2? min 4?)
- Who fixes it:    (Rollo himself / the Magician / nobody; how much does the adult step in?)
- The ending:      (a lesson / a joke / just a calm close)
- The sound:       (narrator pacing, where music sits, what makes noise, what stays silent)
- The look:        (how little moves, camera, how much empty space)
- Steal this:      (the ONE thing Mipo should take from it)
```

## Part 2: Mipo Shorts, Season 1 (one card per episode idea)

Aim for 6 to 8 episodes. Copy this block for each.

```
### S1E<n>: <title>
- Trouble (4-year-old test): (one sentence a 4-year-old gets)
- Verb:          (exactly ONE: wash / dress / feed / stack / share ...)
- Silly turn:
- Resolution beat:
- As a game:     (what the kid's finger does; a second verb = a different episode)
- As a video:    (what's on screen in 60-90s when it's played well; play makes the short)
- Cast:          (Mipo family BLOB/SPUD/TALL/TWIG/CHUNK/WIDE...; a Magician-type helper?)
```

### S1E1: The Bathhouse
- **Trouble (4-year-old test):** "Wash the mud blobs to discover what's hiding inside."
- **Verb:** WASH
- **Silly turn:** *(open: what absurd thing could happen mid-scrub? the mud fights back, the Mipo is ticklish, the bubbles carry it off...)*
- **Resolution beat:** REVEAL: a uniquely generated, named Mipo pops out of the mud.
- **As a game:** scrub with a finger until the mud is gone (`APP-1-BATHHOUSE-PLAN.md`).
- **As a video:** one mud blob arrives, gets scrubbed, the reveal lands, the Mipo reacts. Every discovery is already a ~20s clip (`STUDIO-STRATEGY.md`).
- **Cast:** a random Mipo from any family; the kid is the attendant (no attendant character).
