# Soapbox — a Mipo in a basket cart rolling down a hill, a knikkerbaan to watch

status: idea

A Mipo in a soapbox cart (zeepkist) rolling down a hill by itself; the child just watches it go,
no buttons. After the bike (docs/BIKE.md, parked: "other vehicles suit better"), whose course,
cable-car lift and wheel pictures it reuses. Spike: `scripts/spikes/soapbox` (`r` a new Mipo,
`w` the next tire picture).

## What's there (2026-11-06)

- **A basket on four pram wheels** (two show), sized from the Mipo: long enough for its legs
  stretched out, walls a third of its height, the floor 1.3 wheels off the ground; heavy, a
  little air drag.
- **The Mipo sits in it** (Nikki: "a little basket where the Mipo sits in, not touching his body
  against the ground under it"): facing right, placed so its lowest point is just above the
  floor; the rider collides with the basket's boards (legs rest on the floor, feet against the
  front board), not with the wheels; hands hold the rope (drawn) but aren't pinned.
- **Bounce** (Nikki: "it feels very damped, and these soap carts aren't"): the hips ride on a
  spring (a wheel joint, up and down in the basket, turning freely; 5 Hz), the course has
  ripples and humps, the air drag is lower (0.35). The rider's muscles are gentle (kp 8, at most
  2 rad/s) and aim at fixed angles in the world (upright, legs forward). The wheels on fixed
  axles (Nikki).
- **Ballast**: the cart weighs twice its rider (its thin boards weighed far less than a Mipo, and
  it tipped over backwards).
- **Drawn**: the basket's side in wood with crayon outline and plank seams, drawn in front of the
  Mipo (and the wheels: OMP rubber tires, crayon spokes), its far side darker behind; the rope.
- **Course and lift** as the bike's, with a longer, gentler run-out (the short steep rise tipped
  the short cart over backwards).

## Tried (2026-11-06)

- Hip joint on a low floor: the body stuck out underneath, dragged, rolled the cart over and over.
- Rider muscles aimed at the cart's angle (legs, then the torso): a loop, cart tilts, target with
  it, the body turns the cart further: over backwards, then over its nose.
- Rider muscles at a standing Mipo's strength (kp 40, 15 rad/s): fighting the rope at the hands,
  the torso shoved its hips every frame and braked the cart: it rocked at the start.
- Hands pinned to the cart: held the bobbing body at an angle, pulled it down its spring till
  it dragged on the ground.
- A weld's softness is only in its turning: a "soft" weld at the hips didn't bob at all.
- Wheels on springs (wheel joints): bouncier, but Nikki wants them pinned to the cart.
